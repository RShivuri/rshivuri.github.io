About Xiquel Group

Xiquel Group is a South African ICT company that is 100% black owned, found in the year 2013 by qualified IT professionals with vivid experience in the ICT industry. The company provides software consulting services to clients in various industries. 

With over 10 years of experience, we have delivered services to both the Public and Private sector organizations worldwide.

Xiquel Group is part of the enterprise supplier development & emerging partner programmes.

Xiquel Group is an official partner to global companies with certified industry specialists under our ranks.

We have been leveraging Microsoft technologies and many others to automate and digitize business processes for organizations to improve how they interact with customers and manage their businesses more efficiently and effectively.
